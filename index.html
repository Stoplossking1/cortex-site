Pilot questions

What rental teams need to know.

Short answers to the questions that determine whether a pilot is operationally useful.

Which calls can Cortex cover?

Any calls your team assigns: every call, normal business hours, after hours, overflow, no-answer, or a temporary backup window. Coverage is your operational choice—not an after-hours-only limitation.

What happens when Cortex cannot verify a rate or availability?

It does not guess. Cortex captures the exact equipment or package, intended use, dates, location, fulfillment need, account, and callback details so an authorized employee can confirm the answer.

How does the 14-day pilot work?

We agree on one call set, one workflow, the escalation rules, and a measurable success criterion. Your team reviews the outcomes before any wider rollout.

Can we keep our existing rental number?

Yes. Call forwarding lets customers continue using the number already on your website, vehicles, storefront, and documents. Your phone system stays in place.

Can Cortex take deposits or connect to our rental system?

Only when the specific payment or system connection has been scoped, built, and approved. Until then, Cortex collects the request and routes the next authorized action without claiming a live integration.
