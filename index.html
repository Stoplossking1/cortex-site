Pilot questions

What rental teams need to know.

Which calls can Cortex cover?+

Every call, business hours, after hours, overflow, no-answer, or a temporary backup window—your team chooses.

What if Cortex cannot verify a rate or availability?+

It does not guess. It captures the request so an authorized employee can confirm the answer.

How does the 14-day pilot work?+

We agree on one call set, one workflow, escalation rules, and a measurable success criterion before wider rollout.

Does Cortex handle email as well as phone?+

Yes. Quote requests and account openings that arrive by email — including attachments and web forms — are read and entered into your rental system the same way assigned calls are.

How does Cortex enter requests into our software?+

Directly. If your system has an API, we use it. If it doesn’t, our AI agents operate the software the same way a trained employee does. Modern, custom, or from the nineties — if it has a screen, Cortex writes into it.
